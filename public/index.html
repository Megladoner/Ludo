A QUICK REFRESHER

How to play.

  • Roll a sixBring one token out of base and onto its starting square.
  • Move around the boardMove one token by the exact number shown on the dice.
  • Find a safe squareStar squares are safe. On other squares, land on an opponent to send them home.
  • Make it homeEnter your colored home lane and roll the exact number to finish.
  • Three sixesThree consecutive sixes end your turn. A six otherwise earns another roll.
