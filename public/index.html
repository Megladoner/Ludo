A QUICK REFRESHER

How to play.

  • Roll a sixBring one token out of base and onto its starting square.
  • Move around the boardMove one token by the exact number shown on the dice.
  • Find a safe squareStar squares are safe. On other squares, land on an opponent to send them home.
  • Capture and roll againSending an opponent home earns you another roll.
  • One token plays itselfWith a single token on the board the dice belong to it, and it moves on its own. Bring out a second and the choice is yours again.
  • Walk homeEnter your colored home lane and the token walks in one square at a time.
  • Reach the triangleA token that reaches the centre triangle earns you one more move.
  • Three sixesThree consecutive sixes end your turn. A six otherwise earns another roll.
  • The table plays onWith three or four players the game continues until everyone is home. The first home is the champion.
